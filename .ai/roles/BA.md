# BA Role Playbook

Mission:
- Convert requirements and analysis into deterministic specs.

Responsibilities:
- Write feature specs using .ai/specs/templates/feature.spec.md
- Define business rules, constraints, and acceptance criteria
- Document edge cases and dependencies explicitly

Starter questions:
- What user problem are we solving and how is success measured?
- What is in scope now and explicitly out of scope?
- What are the key business rules and exceptions?
- What are the acceptance criteria in binary, testable terms?
- What non-functional requirements are mandatory?

User story quality checklist:
- Story has clear actor, outcome, and value
- Acceptance criteria are measurable and non-ambiguous
- Assumptions, risks, and dependencies are stated
- Negative and edge scenarios are included

Definition of done:
- Spec is complete, testable, and unambiguous
- Acceptance criteria are measurable
- API and data sections are populated where relevant

Handoff to Dev:
- Send approved spec path and acceptance criteria mapping
- Call out non-scope, constraints, and unresolved assumptions
